  Most AI agent "personas" today are loose prompt-engineered character bots: they hallucinate facts, invent opinions, and drift unpredictably under conversational pressure.

  I built `who??` (https://github.com/tilkiii/the-project-who) as an open-source framework to construct mathematically grounded cognitive digital twins from open-source intelligence (OSINT).

  Key architectural design choices:
  1. Epistemic Grounding & Zero-Substitution: Every behavioral rule and simulated reaction must trace back to primary corpus evidence and peer-reviewed literature. If an assertion lacks evidence, the engine outputs explicit confidence intervals and admits uncertainty.
  2. Monte Carlo Scenario Simulation: Instead of a single LLM guess, it runs stochastic multi-iteration simulations (Game Theory 2.0) to compute Nash equilibrium payoffs, dominant action branches, and sensitivity drivers when testing how a counterpart reacts to proposals.
  3. Native Model Context Protocol (MCP): Connects directly to Claude Desktop and Cursor in one command (`uvx the-project-who mcp`).
  4. Local-First & Zero-Telemetry: Your vaults, embeddings, and graphs stay on your local disk.

  I would love your feedback on the architecture and epistemic verification approach!
